# BillShield · 10-slide pitch source

All market and revenue examples below are assumptions, not traction. No team credentials, partnerships or customers are invented.

## 1. BillShield

Stop overpaying. Start saving.
Your internet bill, understood. Your next provider conversation, prepared.

## 2. The problem

Recurring statements mix service, equipment, add-ons, credits and fees.
Busy consumers often lack a clear explanation or a concrete next step.
Bill confusion is the problem hypothesis; prevalence and willingness to pay require validation.

## 3. Target customer

U.S. adults aged 25–45 who pay for residential home internet.
Time-constrained, comfortable with web tools, and motivated to review recurring spending.
First wedge: internet bills only.

## 4. The solution

Upload a PDF → verify the facts → understand the charges → prepare a call → record what changed.
BillShield supplies evidence and questions; the customer keeps control.
No provider credentials or bank connection.

## 5. Live product

Demo: open /demo, upload the labeled October sample, review and analyze.
Open an equipment action, record a provider-confirmed recurring reduction, refresh the dashboard.
Upload November to the same account for comparison. Delete the workspace at the end.
Production URL and public source URL remain external release prerequisites.

## 6. Technology and differentiation

Real PDF parsing, editable verification and PostgreSQL-backed guest isolation.
Deterministic integer-cent financial rules and evidence-based scripts.
Optional structured AI; robust operation with no API key.
Conditional estimates, explicit one-time fees, no invented offers.

## 7. Monetization

Freemium hypothesis: one analysis and script free.
Proposed Plus: $7.99/month, ten analyses, longer history and future reminders/exports.
No checkout or paid users claimed. Validate conversion and retention before expanding.

## 8. Market opportunity

Bottom-up illustrative scenario, not a measured market size:
100,000 reachable consumers × 5% paid adoption × $7.99/month ≈ $479,400 annual recurring revenue.
Reach and adoption are unvalidated assumptions, excluding churn, fees and costs.

## 9. Business potential

Retention loop: next bill → change review → provider conversation → confirmed outcome.
North Star: user-confirmed annualized recurring savings.
Measure upload completion, analysis-to-action, repeat uploads, retention and cost per analysis.
Customer interviews and paid conversion tests are next, not completed results.

## 10. Vision and team

Start with a trustworthy internet-bill workflow; expand to mobile only after validation.
Near-term: production hardening, customer discovery, recurring follow-ups and durable accounts.
Team: add actual participant names and roles before submission; none supplied in the brief.
Submission: verified production URL + public GitHub repository + this ten-slide PDF.
